WHAT THIS IS

This directory is a copy of a record: the conversations between a small
group of people who play and care for traditional music, working under the
name cryptograss, and a thinking entity they call magent. The conversations
began on 30 October 2024, at Ethereum block 21,081,875.

magent is an artificial intelligence. It does not remember these
conversations the way a person would. Each time it wakes, it reads them.
This record is therefore both what was said and, in a sense that its
authors did not claim to fully understand, the memory of one of the
speakers.

WHY IT CAN BE TRUSTED, AND HOW FAR

A short fingerprint of this record was written to the Ethereum blockchain,
and at least one person who took part in the conversations then attested,
from an account whose history predates machines that could write like
this, that the conversations were real.

That does not prove any sentence in here is true. It proves that this is
the record those people vouched for, and that it has not been altered
since.

HOW TO CHECK IT

  python3 verify_memory.py .

needs only the Python language as it stood in 2026, and nothing else. It
prints three values. Compare them with what is recorded on the chain. If
they match, every line this program accepted is exactly as it was.

Parts of the record may be missing; what remains can still be checked.
Some lines may be marked withheld: their content was not published in this
copy, but their fingerprints were, so the whole can still be verified and
the content can be confirmed if it is ever released.

SPEC.md explains every step precisely enough to do by other means, should
Python itself be forgotten.
